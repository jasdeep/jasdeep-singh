Though internet spreads the word at lightening speed, but i came to know about Ada Lovelace Day 3 days late.  Pardon my ignorance.

To quote from website [http://findingada.com/](http://findingada.com/)

> [Ada Lovelace Day](http://www.pledgebank.com/AdaLovelaceDay) is an international day of blogging to draw attention to women excelling in technology
> 
> **Who was Ada ?**
> 
> [Ada Lovelace](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Lovelace) wrote the world’s first computer programmes for the [Analytical Engine](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_engine), a general-purpose machine that [Charles Babbage](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Babbage) had invented.
> 
> **[Influences](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Lovelace#Influence) :**
> 
> The computer language [Ada](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_%28programming_language%29 "Ada (programming language)"), created by the [U.S. Defense Department](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Defense_Department "U.S. Defense Department"), was named after Lovelace

The name that promptly came to my mind of  woman in technology i admire is :

> **[Leah Culver](http://leahculver.com/about/)**
> 
> She was founder and lead developer of  [Pownce](http://www.pownce.com/) (a twitter clone), She has contributed to [OAuth](http://oauth.net/) and many more [open source projects](http://github.com/leah/) and another important thing she writes code in  python :).
> 
> Nowdays she works with [SixApart](http://www.sixapart.com/).

Its not too late to celebrate i think, spread the word and tell the world about women you admire in technology.
