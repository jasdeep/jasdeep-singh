ਤੂ ਸ਼ਾਹੀਂ ਹੈ ਪਰਵਾਜ਼ ਹੈ ਕਾਮ ਤੇਰਾ ਤੇਰੇ ਸਾਮਨੇ ਆਸਮਾਂ ਔਰ ਭੀ ਹੈਂ ਇਸ ਰੋਜ਼-ਓ-ਸ਼ਬ ਮੇਂ ਉਲਝਕਰ ਨ ਰਿਹ ਜਾ ਕਿ ਤੇਰੇ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ-ਓ-ਮਕਾਂ ਔਰ ਭੀ ਹੈਂ ਸਿਤਾਰੋਂ ਸੇ ਆਗੇ ਜਹਾਂ ਔਰ ਭੀ ਹੈਂ ਅਭੀ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਕੇ ਇਮਤਹਾਂ ਔਰ ਭੀ ਹੈਂ

ਸ਼ਾਹੀਂ -- ਬਾਜ਼, ਰੋਜ਼-ਓ-ਸ਼ਬ --ਦਿਨ-ਰਾਤ

You are a falcon, it is your nature to fly For you there are other skies beyond this sky Don't let the web of dawns and dusks enmesh you For you have other lives,other goal to pursue Beyond these starts are worlds yet to unearth There are more trials of love to test your worth. \[ English translation by [Khushwant Singh](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khushwant_singh) \]

This Urdu Poem is written by [Muhammad Iqbal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iqbal), A great [Urdu](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) poet of 20'th century , He was a staunch [Islamist](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist) and supporter of separate [muslim](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) state ( [Pakistan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) ) during [Indian independence struggle](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement) from the [British Empire](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) . He is considered Shayar-e-Mashriq (Poet of the East ) in <a Literary world .
