The [Sprouted Delhi BarCamp 5](http://jasdeepsingh.wordpress.com/2008/10/10/delhi-barcamp-5/) was exhilarating an experience, Although it was not as glittering as expected from the sponsorships and registrations.

[![](2008-10-14_delhi-barcamp5_halflogo-featured.webp "halflogo")](http://jasdeepsingh.files.wordpress.com/2008/10/halflogo.png)

The formal/informal inauguration was done by the [behind the scenes slide show](http://www.slideshare.net/vineesh1/the-making-of-barcamp-delhi-5-pictualize-presentation?type=powerpoint) and video by [picturulize](http://pictualize.com/) featuring the key guys behind this wonderful unconference.

Then the talks/sessions proceeded, I attended a talk on [Pylons](http://pylonshq.com/) and [Schevo](http://www.schevo.org/) , It seems Pylons might be better than [django](http://www.djangoproject.com/) in terms of modularity, but django has become a rockstar for [python](http://www.python.org/) community and  django has much more active community than pylons.

The other talk i really liked was about [Building a scalable architecture of web applications](http://wiki.directi.com/display/DEV/Building+a+Scalable+Architecture+for+Web+Apps+-+Part+I) by [Bhavin Turakhia](http://bhavin.directi.com/), CEO [Directi](http://directi.com/), It was very much power packed and a must see for any web developer.

The talk of the day was [The Global Financial Crisis and India](http://www.slideshare.net/pjain/the-financial-crisis-and-india-presentation/) by [Pankaj](http://twitter.com/pjain) from [Teknatus](http://www.teknatus.com/), It was very interactive talk and if we can get the video of the talk that would be far more informative, It tells about the harsh realities India might face in near future. Some keypoints of the talks were :

*   Indian Economy is very much integrated  with global economy, so it is prone to be hit by global slowdown, we may see its consequences in coming six months or so.
*   30 years back Indian saved 70 percent of there income, These days it has gone down to 11 percent. So the if the economic disaster strikes, It is going to be a nightmare for many.
*   The US financial crisis is result of sheer ignorance of US policy makers and same can happen to India

This presentation was a big hit with the listeners, it got extended to 90 minutes session from an alloted 30 minutes. The day ended with the coyright infringement talk by [twilightfairy](http://twitter.com/twilightfairy) and how she fought with mainstream media big wig [TOI](http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/) for her rights .

The second day of barcamp started with an interactive discussion about blogging and as the participants arrived ,There was a talk by Barcamp4 and Barcamp5 Coordinator [Piyush](http://silicontryst.wordpress.com/) about Entrepreneurs and Marriages, as most of the Entrepreneurs face matrimonial issues , There was very healthy discussion about it .

Fabian form [Ubuntu](http://www.ubuntu.com/) told the audience about its [importance](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/Fabi%C3%A1nRodr%C3%ADguez?action=AttachFile&do=view&target=20081013-Ubuntu-FabianRodriguez-BarCampDelhi.zip). A hackathon was called on by two young hackers [Pratul](http://pratul.in/) and [Parteek](https://twitter.com/prtksxna) , but it turned out to be a ubuntu installation and  support session for newbies.

Nikhil from [Medianama](http://www.medianama.com/), interacted with enterprenures about there ventures. The day ended with a call to change the conversation to 'Poverty' on [blog action day](http://blogactionday.org/) by [simply arun](http://simplyarun.com/).

You can search youtube and slide share with tag bcd5 for videos and slides respectively.
