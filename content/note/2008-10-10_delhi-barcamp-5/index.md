The [Delhi Barcamp4](http://jasdeepsingh.wordpress.com/2008/05/19/delhi-barcamp-4-take-aways/) was quite an eye opener and great learning experience for me as it was first [Barcamp](http://barcamp.org/). To quote from [DelhiBarCamp5](http://blog.barcampdelhi.com/) blog :

> **The seeds have germinated. A lot of sunlight and some water sprinkles have softened the outer shell. Little sprouts can be seen carving out themselves.**

Lets  see wats in store for this sprouted  barcamp, as for me my first barcamp as landed me into  job work with [RouteGuru.com](http://routeguru.com/) as I was tested positive with [EBC's(Entrepreneurial Blood Cells)](http://routeguru.com/careers) (Really ?).

Looking at the [sponsors](http://barcampdelhi.com/) list and [talks/sessions](http://wiki.barcampdelhi.com/index.php?title=BCD5Sessions) , it seems it is going to be big. I am looking forward for the sessions on [Python programming](http://www.python.org/) , [Terracotta](http://www.terracotta.org/) and of course lots of [Entrepreneurship](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurship) gyan.

Check the [venue details here](http://barcampdelhi.com/?page_id=104) or simply sms "**rg <your location in delhi > to bcd5**" to 56767 to arrive at the barcamp reception.

[![](2008-10-10_delhi-barcamp-_barcamp-poster-featured.webp "barcamp-poster")](http://jasdeepsingh.files.wordpress.com/2008/10/barcamp-poster.jpg)

**Place :** IIT Delhi Seminar Hall Main Admin Block

**Date** : 11th and 12th October (Saturday-Sunday)

**Timings** : From 10:00 AM to 6.00 PM (these are ought to be stretched as it is an unconference)

and yes we will get a free tshirt and free lunch there :)

so join the fun !
