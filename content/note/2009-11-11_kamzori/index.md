**ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰੀਆਂ**

ਤੇਰੀ ਕੋਈ ਨਹੀਂ ਸੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਇੱਕ ਸੀ ਮੈਂ ਕਰਦਾ ਸੀ ਪਿਆਰ

[Read it in roman script](http://en.girgit.chitthajagat.in/parchanve.wordpress.com/2009/08/27/kamzori/)

**Weaknesses**

You had none.

I had one.

I loved.

**Source:** A friend shared this poem by famous playwright and poet [Bertolt Brecht](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertolt_Brecht)

Punjabi Translation is by yours truly.
